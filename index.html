🧺 Conservatory Picnic Booth

Picnic on the Glass

Play with a misty greenhouse window overlooking a sunny picnic meadow. Camera frames are processed on this device and never uploaded.

  • 😮 Make an "O" mouth pose to breathe vapor/fog onto the glass.
  • 👌 Pinch thumb & index finger then extend your index finger to clear fog; pinch back to stop (or spread palm to wipe).
  • 📸 Press Photo (or Space) to snap a framed picnic keepsake.
  • Pond: point to guide the fish. Bring all five fingertips together like a beak, hold, then release to drop another portion. Other hands scare them away. Use Play and Drop fish food without a camera.
